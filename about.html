About North Star Bakery

Our Story

Our bakery began with a simple idea: good bread should be part of everyday life. We bake for neighbors on their way to work, families stopping by, and people planning celebrations.

Our Sourcing Philosophy

We choose ingredients carefully and bake in small batches to keep our shelves fresh. If you have a food allergy, please ask us about ingredients before ordering.

Meet the Team

Our bakers start early with the breads and pastries. At the counter, our team helps customers choose their favorites and arrange pickups.
